# Logging in to DelftBlue

DelftBlue is TU Delft's high-performance computing system. You connect to it remotely from your computer.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Connect to DelftBlue using a terminal or SSH client
- Recognize a successful login
- Verify your username and current computer
- Log out safely

## Before you begin

You need:

- An active TU Delft NetID
- Permission to access DelftBlue (request via [DelftBlue Access Request Form](https://tudelft.topdesk.net/tas/public/ssp/content/serviceflow?unid=5eecdac603ab4ca896e162836a215d64))
- An internet connection
- Access to the TU Delft network or eduVPN
- An SSH client
