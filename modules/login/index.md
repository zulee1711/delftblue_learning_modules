# Logging in to DelftBlue

DelftBlue is TU Delft's high-performance computing system. You connect to it remotely from your computer.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Connect to DelftBlue using a terminal or SSH client
- Recognize a successful login
- Verify your username and current computer
- Log out safely

## Before you begin

You need:

- An active TU Delft NetID
- An internet connection
- Permission to access DelftBlue

### Check your network connection

When you are on campus, connect through the TU Delft network.

When you are off campus, connect to the TU Delft network using [eduVPN](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/student/my-study-me/study-tools/remote-services) through **Institute Access**.
